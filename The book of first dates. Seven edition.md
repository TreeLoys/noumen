## 2. Dictionary — 26 words + 3 auxiliary

**FACT** — a distinction established for a perspective: the same in all its admissible variants, with non-empty K. Not necessarily the chosen world.

**TOKEN** — a distinguishable sign without a binding. Has not yet managed to become an answer.

**QUESTION** — an operation that partitions configurations into admitted and rejected. Two depths: the value of the feature is unknown; the question is not constructed.

**ANSWER** — a token bound to a question, a rule, and a position: Y_{P,R}(q).

**ADMISSIBLE SET (K)** — configurations compatible with all accepted conditions. Storage — an epistemic [CHOICE].

**FEATURE** — a function on configurations. What exactly is being asked.

**INVARIANT** — a feature, the same on all elements of a non-empty K. An established fact.

**LIMITATION** — an invariant of the form "in G there is no ω with feature f". A full-fledged answer, [SOLVED] in G; not MU, not emptiness.

**RULE** — in a model: a declared operation; in the world: what has repeated, noticed after the fact. Mixing the addressees — smuggling.

**REFERENCE STANDARD** — a declared position or state against which things are checked. **CORRECTNESS** — coincidence with the reference standard; a property of a pair, not of an answer by itself.

**PERSPECTIVE** *(auxiliary)* — a role with inputs, ways of answering, and identity through changes. A coordinate is not a perspective.

**EXECUTOR (POSITION OF EXECUTION, `this`)** — the context of execution, which introduces spaces, rules, reference standards, and pays for friction. Not an element of the registry it compiles: a program does not contain its own execution. To ask about the executor is lawful; to answer within the registry — is not.

**RESOURCE POSITION** — the same `this` in the budget role: the source of the cost of verifications and the owner of decisions not to verify. A given: a position's budget is not derived from the position.

**DENOUMENIZATION** — a local change after which a contrast begins to work stably for a perspective. **One act, two poles.** The noetic pole — a change of attitude, the cessation of reification: the feature no longer counts as a thing. The noematic pole — the appearance of a stable contrast: what was noise becomes signal. *Parser tuning: noesis — raw bytes ceased to count as "data in general"; noema — a stable temperature contrast appeared.* Mixing the poles, or counting only one — smuggling. Ten steps — from presentation to public ratification; one step does not guarantee the next.

**RENOUMENIZATION** — an expansion of K or a loss of a feature, all the way down to the question.

**RELATIVE NOUMENON** — a feature that varies between the admissible variants of a perspective. Not an object — a dimension.

**ABSOLUTE NOUMENON** — [LABEL]: a prohibition on including the absence of the entire frame of distinction in the list of its distinguishable elements. Not a variant, not K=Ω, not empty K, not a class of collapse. Notation: MU_G(q).

**MU** — an active diagnosis: question q applies an object-level operation beyond the domain of grammar G. Not a third value of truth, not "unknown", not silence. Active — it removes the question with an operation; distinguish it from the boundary of expressibility, which is passive: where there is nothing to say, nothing is said there; here — the question is removed, with a date. If all four answers to a question are rejected — yes, no, both, neither — it is not the answer that is broken but the question: a fifth value is not introduced; MU is introduced.

**COEXISTENCE BEFORE SELECTION** *(formerly "everyone dances" — the word is in the labels)* — when K consists of several configurations and there is no selector, none is appointed actual; they are stored together in the list.

**SELECTION** — the act of appointing the actual variant. The selector is declared; without declaration — smuggling of choice.

**FRICTION** *(journal)* — the cost of verifying or maintaining a fact. A journal row: verifying X costs Y to resource Z; the value of X — W; the decision — to verify / tagged heuristic until the review date. There are no empty rows; the budget owner is always named.

**MOVE** — an order of a special kind: a position carrying the capacity for the next one. A generative account of the move — a [CHOICE] of style.
